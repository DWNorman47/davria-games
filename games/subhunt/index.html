Sub Hunt

Silent submarine duel · everyone on their own phone

How to play

  • 2–4 players, each on their own phone. Your sub is invisible to the others.
  • Each turn has two steps: first Move one square or Wait, then Fire a torpedo, Ping sonar, or call it Done.
  • Torpedoes run straight to the edge and sink the first sub they hit — but the full trail, including your firing position, is revealed to everyone and fades after a round.
  • Sonar ping reveals every enemy sub on your map — but your own position echoes back to everyone. Contacts (dashed outlines) are snapshots and fade after a round; subs may have moved!
  • Turbulent water spreads inward from the edges; subs caught in it are visible to all.
  • Wrecks block torpedoes. One hit sinks you. Last sub standing wins!
