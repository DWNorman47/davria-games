How to play

  • Copycat: choose from a few offered patches, memorize one, then repaint it after the picture vanishes. Harder patches multiply your points, and every result gets a side-by-side reveal.
  • Freestyle: optionally choose a creative directive, then take turns adding limited ink to one shared canvas. Each painter can undo or clear their own turn.
  • Telephone: choose a prompt pack, pass prompts through alternating drawings and guesses, then react to and save each revealed chain.
