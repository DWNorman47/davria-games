Rail Routes

Claim railways across North America · 2–8 players on their own phones

Open this on a TV or laptop to show the whole board to the room. It doesn't take a seat.

How to play

  • 2–8 players, each on their own phone. A TV display is optional.
  • On your turn do one thing: draw 2 train cards (a face-up locomotive counts as both), claim a route by paying cards that match its color and length, or draw 3 destination tickets and keep at least 1.
  • Locomotives are wild. Gray routes take any one color.
  • Routes score 1, 2, 4, 7, 10, 15 points for 1–6 trains.
  • Double and triple tracks: 2–3 players can use one track per route, 4–5 players two, 6–8 players all three. You can't own two tracks of the same route.
  • When someone ends a turn with 2 or fewer trains, everyone gets one final turn.
  • Finished tickets add their points, unfinished ones subtract them. The longest continuous railway earns +10.
